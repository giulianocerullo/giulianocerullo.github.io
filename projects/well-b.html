WELL-B Wellness App Concept

Context
Universidad Villanueva • Consumer Behavior
Type
Academic Project
Period
Sep 2024 – Dec 2024

Overview

Final project for the Consumer Behaviour course at Universidad Villanueva in Madrid, developed during my exchange semester. I designed WELL-B, a concept for a wellness app that tracks six health domains and guides users through LIVIA, a personal life and vitality coach.

The project was delivered in three outputs:

Video YouTube linkPromotional video presenting the app and its value propositionOpen Report .pdf fileProject report explaining the concept, target, positioning and business modelOpen PDF Brand and UI image galleryBrand and UI assets: logos, iPhone mockups and icon setView gallery

Key Tasks

  • Defined the concept and target user, applying consumer behaviour frameworks such as Maslow’s hierarchy of needs, the AIDA model and gamified loyalty
  • Developed the full brand identity in Adobe Illustrator, from the WELL-B and LIVIA logos to lettering and colour palette
  • Designed the app’s UI/UX, iPhone mockups and icon set in Figma
  • Edited the promotional video in Final Cut Pro X
  • Built the business model: free core app with a €4.99 monthly premium tier
  • Aligned the concept with the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDG 3, 4, 10, 13)

Core Insights

One app, the whole wellbeing journey: six health domains in a single experience, with a personal coach that turns tracking into guidance

Communication follows the user, not the technology: AI powers the personalisation, but customer-facing messages focus on benefits rather than on the technology itself

Freemium for adoption, premium for engagement: a free entry point lowers the barrier, while gamified loyalty drives the upgrade
