EssilorLuxottica Digital Strategy Review

Context
EM Normandie Business School • Digital, Data & AI Transformation Strategy
Type
Academic Project
Period
Sep 2026 – Oct 2026

Overview

Comparative strategy case on EssilorLuxottica, assessing how the group’s digital initiatives, data assets, IT architecture and AI capabilities support its strategic pillars, and proposing a 2027-30 transformation roadmap.

The project was delivered in two outputs:

Slide deck .pdf fileSlide deck covering business, digital, data, architecture and AI strategy, with gap analysis, recommendations and roadmapOpen PDF Simulator interactive web appInteractive investment simulator to test the cost and payback of the proposed roadmapOpen

Key Tasks

  • Mapping of four digital initiatives and seven data sources across consumers, partners and patients
  • Strategic alignment matrix linking initiatives, data and IT to the group’s strategic pillars
  • Gap analysis across data integration, IT architecture and AI adoption
  • Risk mapping of the proposed transformation moves
  • Costed 2027-30 roadmap with an interactive investment simulator

Core Insights

Fragmented data foundation: two ERPs with no group-wide data layer limit integration across the value chain

AI gap in operations: no AI deployed in supply chain or back-office, despite strong consumer-facing initiatives

Limited reach and data control: partner platform available only in the US, and smart-glasses data held by Meta

Proposed roadmap: four moves, from ERP unification to new data-sharing terms with Meta, in a ~€0.5-0.8bn 2027-30 plan, with payback tested through the investment simulator.
