AirGuard Shopify Store

Context
Universidad Villanueva • E-Commerce Business Game
Type
Academic ProjectEntrepreneurial
Period
Sep 2024 – Dec 2024

Overview

E-Commerce Business Game held at Universidad Villanueva in Madrid during my exchange semester. In a team of seven, we created AirGuard, a dropshipping brand of AirPods cases, and ran it live for three months, donating the full profit to Save the Children Spain.

The project was delivered as a final presentation covering brand, marketing plan, logistics, results and lessons learned.

Presentation .pdf fileFinal presentation: brand, marketing plan, logistics, results and lessons learnedOpen PDF

Key Tasks

  • Built the brand identity in house with Adobe Illustrator, from logo to social media assets
  • Launched the Shopify store on a custom domain, with integrated shipping management and real-time order tracking
  • Planned the social media strategy on Instagram and TikTok: weekly posts and videos, stories, paid ads and a giveaway contest to drive reach
  • Managed suppliers and fulfilment, switching from SHEIN to AliExpress after test orders revealed unreliable shipping

Core Insights

31 units sold at a ~40% margin per unit

~530 monthly website visits and 100+ Instagram followers on a €20 advertising budget

€84 profit donated to Save the Children Spain

Top grade in the class 🏅

Lessons learned: the supplier change cost time early on, and spreading efforts over two social platforms diluted results. Focusing on one channel and securing a reliable supplier from the start would have accelerated growth.
